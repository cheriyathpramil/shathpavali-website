Privacy Policy for Shathpavali

Last Updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shathpavali ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our iOS application. By using Shathpavali, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. HealthKit Data (Strict Privacy)

Shathpavali integrates with Apple's HealthKit to read and write health and fitness data (such as Steps, Active Energy, Blood Glucose, and Walk Workouts).

  • Your Data Remains Yours: We do not sell, rent, or share your HealthKit data with any third parties for advertising, marketing, or data mining purposes.
  • On-Device Priority: The vast majority of your health data remains locally on your device.
  • Apple Intelligence & Siri: Any integration with Siri or Apple Intelligence processes your health data on your device or via Apple's Private Cloud Compute. We do not send your health data to third-party AI models (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic).

2. Family Sharing & Cloud Storage

If you explicitly choose to create or join a "Family Group", certain limited progress data (such as meal walk completion status, step counts, and optionally blood sugar levels) will be synced to our secure backend (powered by Supabase) so your family members can see your progress.

  • Opt-In Only: Data is only sent to our servers if you actively log into an account and join a Family Group.
  • Encryption: Data transmitted to and from our servers is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Right to Delete: You can delete your account and all associated Family Sharing data at any time from within the app.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your Family Sharing data only for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all personal data stored on our servers is permanently deleted. Health data stored in Apple HealthKit must be managed or deleted directly through the Apple Health app on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Supabase for authentication and database management strictly for the Family Sharing feature. Supabase acts as our data processor and is bound by strict confidentiality and GDPR-compliant data processing agreements.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app and updating the "Last Updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact the developer team.
