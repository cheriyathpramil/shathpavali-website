Privacy & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 5, 2026

At ShathPavali (the "App"), your privacy is our core principle. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, protect, and handle your data, ensuring strict compliance with Apple's HealthKit guidelines and global privacy standards.

HIPAA & Data Privacy Commitment

While ShathPavali is a consumer wellness application and may not strictly fall under the legal definition of a "Covered Entity" under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), we have architected our data infrastructure following strict HIPAA technical safeguards. Your health data is treated as Protected Health Information (PHI). We utilize industry-standard encryption (at rest and in transit) and strict access controls to ensure your data remains confidential.

1. Information We Collect

A. Health Data (HealthKit & Manual Entry)

If you grant permission, the App reads and writes data to your device's Apple Health app (HealthKit). This includes:

  • Steps & Walking Data: To track your post-meal walks and evaluate goal completion.
  • Blood Glucose Data: If you choose to log blood sugar readings, we read/write this to HealthKit.
Strict HealthKit Data Policy: We DO NOT use HealthKit data for marketing, advertising, or data mining. We DO NOT sell this data to third-party data brokers. HealthKit data remains primarily on your device.

B. Siri & Apple Intelligence

ShathPavali integrates with Siri and Apple Intelligence to allow voice commands and semantic queries.

  • On-Device Processing: When you ask Siri for your steps or blood sugar, the query is processed securely on your device or via Apple's Private Cloud Compute.
  • No Third-Party AI Models: We DO NOT send your health data to third-party Large Language Models (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini). Your health data remains within the Apple ecosystem.

C. Family Sharing & Cloud Sync (Supabase)

If you explicitly choose to create an account and join a "Family Group", limited progress data (such as meal walk completion and optionally step counts) will be synced to our secure backend (powered by Supabase) so your family can see your progress.

  • Opt-In Only: Data is only sent to our servers if you actively log into an account and join a Family Group.
  • Encryption: Data transmitted to and from our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest.

D. Payment and Subscription Data

ShathPavali operates using Apple's in-app purchase infrastructure. We DO NOT collect, process, or store your credit card or billing details. Apple handles all transactions.

2. Where Your Data is Stored

  • Local Storage (Guest Mode): If you are not logged in, all walk history, meal schedules, and blood sugar readings are stored strictly locally in your device's secure file system.
  • Cloud Sync (Supabase): If you create an account, your data is securely synced to our backend infrastructure hosted on Supabase.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to third parties. We only share data with trusted infrastructure providers (like Supabase for database hosting and Apple HealthKit for local health data integration) strictly for the purpose of operating the App. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality and GDPR-compliant data processing agreements.

4. Security Measures

We implement robust, HIPAA-inspired technical safeguards:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Data stored on our servers is encrypted at rest.
  • Access Control: Row-Level Security (RLS) is strictly enforced on our database. Your data can only be queried by your authenticated user ID.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the App settings. Upon deletion, your personal data and health records are permanently scrubbed from our active servers immediately.
  • HealthKit Data: Health data stored in Apple HealthKit must be managed or deleted directly through the Apple Health app on your device.

6. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have complete control over your data:

  • Right to Access: You can view all your logged data within the App.
  • Right to Erasure/Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated health data directly within the App settings.
  • Right to Revoke Access: You can revoke Apple HealthKit permissions at any time via your iOS Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices.
  • Right to Know: We disclose all data collection practices fully within this policy. We do not sell your personal information.
